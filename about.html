About Me

I am an engineering physics student focusing on mechatronics. I lean toward a hands-on, hardware-first approach—combining first-principles analytical logic with practical prototyping, whether that means machining aluminum or routing a custom PCB.

When I step away from CAD and code, you can usually find me outside. I spend my downtime trail running, surfing, and trying to keep my garden alive. I like keeping things simple, both in design and outdoors.
